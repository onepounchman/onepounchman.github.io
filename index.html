Hi!

Welcome! I am an Applied Scientist on the Accio team at Alibaba, working on agentic post-training. Previously, I interned at Amazon, Meta, and IQVIA.

I received my PhD in Statistics from the University of California, Irvine, advised by Prof. Hengrui Cai. I previously studied Biostatistics as a master's student at the University of Washington and received my BSc in Applied Mathematics from Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing capable and trustworthy language models and agents. I am particularly interested in:

  • Agentic post-training, with a focus on long-horizon reinforcement learning, supervised fine-tuning, and on-policy distillation.
  • Evaluation: reliable and cost-efficient evaluation of LLM agents.
  • Trustworthy AI: uncertainty quantification and interpretability.
